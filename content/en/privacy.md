The site has no login, no advertising and no cookies.

**Visit counting.** We count visits with [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/). GoatCounter does not use cookies and does not store IP addresses or other personal data.

**The games** run in your browser. Answers and scores are not sent to us.

**Fonts.** The Laksekapteinen game loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser then sends your IP address to Google.

**Hosting.** The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the domain uses Cloudflare. These services may keep technical logs.
