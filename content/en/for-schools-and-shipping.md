The games on Captain PhD are free to play, with no login and no ads. They are meant to stay that way.

## Open content, paid service

Much of the content is based on the open textbooks from [Marfag](https://www.marfag.no/), licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0. That means questions and texts adapted from Marfag are shared under the same licence, and anyone may use them with attribution. So I do not sell the content. What can be arranged for payment is the work around the games.

## What can be arranged

These are not finished products, but offers I can build by agreement.

- **A class version with your own look.** The game with the name and logo of your school, academy or shipping company, for use in teaching or training.
- **Your own question set and results view.** Questions adapted to your course or routines, and a simple view of the results for a class or a crew group.
- **Sponsorship.** A thank-you line on the game card and in the game, so that a game can stay free for everyone. A sponsor cannot influence the content.

## How I work

- Every rule number, light colour, angle, sound signal and figure is checked against the source, and the source is stated in each game.
- The games do not replace official examinations or training.
- If the book and the current regulations disagree, that is stated.
- Price and delivery time are agreed case by case. I do not publish prices here.
